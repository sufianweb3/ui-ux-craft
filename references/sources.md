## Round 2 sources (read 2026-10-18)

Short-names in the first column are how rules cite these sources across the skill (for example "Apple HIG Alerts", "Apple 227", "Emil break-ui"). **Authority** levels: **primary** = the organisation's own published guidance about its own platform or product; **practitioner** = a recognised expert's own first-party rules; **secondary** = educational or marketing content whose general design claims were cross-checked (and sometimes rejected, see `conflicts.md` #15); **community/unverified** = not authoritative for facts, used only for structure or as flagged patterns.

Apple's HIG pages were read through the machine-readable endpoint `https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/design/human-interface-guidelines/<slug>.json` (useful for future refreshes); the human URL is `https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/<slug>`. Change-log dates are given where the page shows one.
